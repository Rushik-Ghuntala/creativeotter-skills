# Voice samples, consent and reuse

CreativeOtter adaptation, 2026-11-01. Follow [the runtime contract](../runtime-contract.md).

Use only explicitly enabled cloning providers. A private recording, speaker permission, current provider consent and approval to spend are separate requirements. Get the current consent text and policy/deletion links from the configured CreativeOtter backend; this repository supplies no legally operative consent form or product policy. Stop cloning if the flow is unavailable. Do not fabricate a policy URL, use a different product's terms, or assume a past checkbox covers a new provider.

For sample preparation, prefer clear speech by one speaker, low background noise and no music or effects. Obtain duration, MIME, size, language and quality requirements from the live provider contract. Do not promise a universal one-minute requirement or provider quota. Use [private uploads](../platform-upload.md) with the required purpose. Never expose the sample through a permanent public URL.

Track the returned consent, Character and voice identifiers. Reuse the approved identity for future synthesis using the clone's actual provider; a paid audition needs its own quote and review. Provider switching, replacing a source sample or creating another provider copy requires the relevant user request and consent. Use current deletion controls when the user requests revocation or removal; do not promise provider-side deletion before it is confirmed.
