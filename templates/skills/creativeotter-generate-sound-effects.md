# Plan sound effects

CreativeOtter adaptation, 2026-11-06. Follow [the runtime contract](references/runtime-contract.md).

CreativeOtter cannot currently generate or render sound effects. State that for an execution request and offer a useful sound brief. Do not use an inherited sound model, pass an audio category to the image-only model catalog, or charge an image quote.

Describe what should be heard: source, action, material, timing, perspective, room character, texture and exclusions. Separate one-shots from looping ambience and musical tracks. For synchronization, use only supplied timings or a proposed cue sheet, clearly described as a plan. Do not claim a finished audio file, listening review or playback. Save a general project brief only if requested and supported; do not invent a sound-effects asset tool.
