# Plan an authorized video recast

CreativeOtter adaptation, 2026-11-06. Follow [the runtime contract](references/runtime-contract.md).

Video recasting and motion transfer are not available in CreativeOtter. Explain that before promising a swap, asking for recordings or preparing paid work. The current image billing/upload flow cannot authorize or import a video swap. Do not call inherited swap, lip-sync or video models.

For a planning request, describe the subject mapping by position and appearance, intended fictional or authorized identity, and what should remain unchanged: scene, wardrobe, camera, timing and soundtrack. Use actual observed content or the user's description. Require appropriate permission for a real person's likeness; a photo alone is not consent.

Use `manage_characters` only to inspect the requested saved identity, and do not claim a saved character has been applied to footage. Provide a recast brief, not a preview or finished video. A new still image is a separate requested deliverable with its own supported workflow.
