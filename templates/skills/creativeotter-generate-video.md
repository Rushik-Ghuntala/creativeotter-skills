# Plan a video clip

CreativeOtter adaptation, 2026-11-06. Follow [the runtime contract](references/runtime-contract.md).

Current CreativeOtter execution is image-only. Even if the host has independent video tools, CreativeOtter has no video credit/execution contract. Do not call those tools as a CreativeOtter video, use an image quote for footage or substitute a paid still image.

If asked to generate, animate, extend or lip-sync a clip, explain that video generation is not available and offer a shot plan or ready production prompt. A requested model must not be silently changed. Do not discover video models through the image-only model catalog or follow inherited provider schemas.

For planning, describe the subject, one main action, camera behavior, duration, ratio, continuity, optional dialogue and ending. Use the actual supplied image/video only as planning context. Use plan-video for saving a film storyboard on request. No queued job, footage, soundtrack or download exists merely because a plan was written.
