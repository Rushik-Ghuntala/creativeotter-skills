# Plan a complete film

CreativeOtter adaptation, 2026-11-06. Follow [the runtime contract](references/runtime-contract.md).

Help turn the brief into an ordered story: audience, goal, script, shots, durations, visual continuity and audio direction. Honor approved decisions and requested review stages. Use plan-video to save the film and ordered storyboard when the user requests persistence, through `manage_projects` and `manage_storyboards`.

Current video, speech, music, processing and final assembly services are unavailable. Say that clearly when a finished film is requested. Do not announce rendering, save invented clip URLs, mark the film complete, or call unsupported film creation/assembly aliases.

Planning does not authorize paid image keyframes. Generate stills only when specifically requested, following the supported image workflow and requested count. Preserve finished assets; a request to diagnose a film problem does not authorize regenerating them.
