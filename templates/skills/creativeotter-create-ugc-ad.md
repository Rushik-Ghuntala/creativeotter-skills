# Plan a creator-style ad

CreativeOtter adaptation, 2026-11-06. Follow [the runtime contract](references/runtime-contract.md).

For script and planning requests, develop a natural hook, product demonstration, supported benefit and call to action. Use product-only narration, a presenter or an unboxing concept as appropriate. Preserve supplied claims and disclosures; do not invent testimonials or performance claims.

If asked for a finished video, explain that CreativeOtter video/audio production is unavailable and offer the script and shot list. Use plan-video and current Studio project/storyboard tools only when saving the plan is requested.

A saved character can guide the plan through `manage_characters` and a brand through `manage_themes`; neither creates a speaking presenter. Do not call voice, lip-sync, video or assembly tools. Product stills require an explicit image request and their own paid image workflow; do not generate them automatically for a planning-only ad.
